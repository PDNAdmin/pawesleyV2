# BRIEF: Forever In Our Heart (Pa. Wesley Olajide Ojo Aiyegboka)

Interviewed (in conversation + one question round), 2026-11-04. Site lives at the project root; this folder holds the brief and lab shots.

## Interview answers (their words where given)

1. **Vibe:** the printed programme, alive. Client: "it's too boring" (v1), and of curo.events "wants the same font from the pdf and color". "Take us through a story."
2. **Journey:** "the exact same order as the pdf. How everything was arranged." Front cover, Funeral Service page, Order of Funeral Services + Church Needs, service items 1 to 17, Biography, Tributes, Photo Speak, back cover (Appreciation).
3. **Energy:** reverent and warm; calm reading pages, one swell at the biography, a quiet held close.
4. **Feeling / one moment:** chosen: **his life journey**. The biography is the peak.
5. **Something no site does:** the photographs of his life develop on the paper as you read his story (signature move, below).
6. **Range:** editorial, the booklet's own maximal print voice (gold, wine, brown, cream dots). Not premium-minimal.
7. **One world or scenes:** "turning the book": chapters, page by page.
8. **Assets:** 32 family photos, the programme PDF (its own artwork extracted: cover cutout, bio cutout, gold silk, wax seal, cross ground, dotted paper, prayer photo, logo). No generation, no spend.

Fonts: lookalikes now (Red Hat Display exact; Playfair Display for Reina Neue; Great Vibes for Ludovica; Anton for FounFree), stacks ready for licensed files.
Film: Remotion memorial film, silent, shareable.

## Brand hard rules (override taste defaults)
- PDF order, PDF colours, PDF type voices. The cream-and-gold palette is the client's brand, not a default reach.
- Verbatim text: tributes and biography exactly as printed. No invented copy in the programme content.
- Readability for elders: gold on cream only as display or fill; gold *text* on cream uses deeper steps of the same hue (#A8670F large, #8C5410 small). Exact #DE992E everywhere it reads (brown grounds, fills, seal, ornaments).

## Feeling curve
1. Recognition: the cover they held in their hands, the portrait rising out of gold silk, the seal pressing down.
2. Reverence: a quiet title page, the church and the day.
3. Orientation: the order of service laid out like a contents page.
4. Participation: reading along with the service, hymns in both tongues.
5. Hush: an empty brown page, one line: Biography of *Late*.
6. **Intimacy (peak):** his life, paragraph by paragraph, photographs developing beside the words, the year rolling from 1943 to 2026.
7. Belonging: thirty-four voices, one after another.
8. Warmth: the faces, travelling past.
9. Tenderness: the film.
10. Gratitude: the back cover. The family's thank-you, held.

**Peak:** "As I read his story, the old photos slowly developed next to the words, and the year kept rolling forward until it reached 2026." (Act 6)
**Tell-someone:** "It's the site where his photos develop beside his life story as you read it."
**Authored silence:** Act 5 (brown interstitial) is deliberate quiet before the peak, not dead scroll.
