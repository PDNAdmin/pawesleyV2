HYMN INSTRUMENTALS
==================

Put the music files in this folder with EXACTLY these names:

  jesus-lives.mp3         Item 3   Hymn: Jesus Lives! (Jesu Ye)
  jerusalem-on-high.mp3   Item 7   Hymn: Jerusalem on High (Jerusalem T'orun)
  hymn-261.mp3            Item 13  Recessional Hymn 261: We speak of the realms of the Blest
  sleep-on-beloved.mp3    Item 15  Sleep on, Beloved (Maa Sun Olufe)
  in-christ-alone.mp3     Item 15  In Christ Alone (Ninu Kristi)
  photo-speak.mp3         Photo Speak section (optional background music)

Tips
- MP3 at 128 to 192 kbps keeps files small for phones in church (about 3 to 5 MB for 4 minutes).
- To use .m4a instead, edit the file names in js/data-audio.js.
- Nothing ever autoplays. A missing file shows "Instrumental coming soon".
- Only upload music you have the rights to share.
