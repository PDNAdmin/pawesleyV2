# Surface brief: index.html (the programme)

Mode: Experience (the work leads), with Read discipline for the service text.
Audience: mourners on phones in church (many elders) and family revisiting as a keepsake, equally.
Task: follow the service, read and hear the hymns, read the life and tributes, see the photos, watch and share the film.
Constraints: PDF order exactly; verbatim text; AA contrast; 48px targets; audio never autoplays.
Memorable moment: the biography darkroom (photos develop as you read, year rolls 1943 to 2026).
Open: client to upload hymn audio (slots: assets/audio/).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The printed programme, re-set as a cinematic film-and-booklet: a dark showreel opening, then calm, generous chapters in the booklet's exact order. Refuses the memorial template (pastel hero, clip-art candle icon, a card grid as the page structure) and refuses the literal print scan (dense dots, crowded centred blocks). Tribute cards in programme order and a drawn candle flame are sanctioned additions (client asked for dynamic tributes and a surprise).

OWN-WORLD: Deep programme brown (#2A1408 to #642900) as the cinematic ground for cover, hush, film and close; warm cream paper (#FBF3E2) for reading chapters, dot pattern only as a 3% whisper at section edges; gold #DE992E as light (rules, numbers, active tab, play), deeper gold for text on cream; wine #7F2923 for liturgy speakers and the selected state. Didone display (Playfair standing in for Reina Neue) at large scale, script (Great Vibes) only for Hymn/Orin/Late moments, Red Hat Display for everything read. Hairline gold rules, square-ish 6px corners, no cards-in-cards.

STORY: Visitors see his face in motion and his name, understand this is the programme, tap a tab to where the service is, read and hear each hymn, then sit with his life, the tributes, the faces, and the family's thanks.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed showreel video (slow graded photo sequence) under a left-weighted brown scrim; logo top-left small; name as three lines of didone, 9vw max, lower-left; dates line; two actions: "Order of service" (gold, primary) and "Watch the film". Sticky tab bar appears at the fold.

FORM: Cinematic programme (candidate 1 of 7, the pick, chosen by the user's delegate on legibility for elders). Raise from the gatefold (assigned): each hymn is a numbered track with a large play control that docks a persistent player bar. seed:066d2c2b282fb745
